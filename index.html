How categories are read: taken directly from your statement's tags, or matched by keyword in the description when a tag is missing.

How the prediction works: average daily spend across the uploaded period, projected to 30 days, with an 80% confidence range based on how variable your daily spending is. With only a few weeks of data this is a rough estimate — it gets more reliable with more months on file.

How flags are chosen: a transaction is flagged when it sits well outside the normal range for its category, using the interquartile spread of your own amounts — no fixed threshold, so it adapts to your spending.

How clusters are formed: K-Means groups every expense into three size bands purely from the amounts themselves — no manual thresholds.

How recurring payments are found: merchants that appear 2 or more times, plus anything tagged as an automatic or subscription payment.
